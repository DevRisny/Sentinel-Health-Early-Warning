# Data sheet: Sentinel prototype data

## 1. Real environmental data

| Field | Source | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tmax`, `tmin` | Open-Meteo Historical Weather (ERA5) | °C | daily |
| `dewpoint_mean`, `rh_mean` | ERA5 | °C, % | daily mean |
| `wind_max` | ERA5 | km/h | daily max at 10 m |
| `wbgt_max_hourly` | derived | index | max over the day of 0.567·T + 0.393·e + 3.94 using same-hour T and dew point |
| `pm10_mean`, `pm10_max`, `pm25_mean`, `dust_mean` | Open-Meteo Air Quality (CAMS global) | µg/m³ | from hourly values |

Coverage: 5 districts × 1,049 days (2024-01-01 to 2026-11-14), 5,245 rows, no missing values. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (attribute Open-Meteo, ECMWF/Copernicus). Fetch: `scripts/fetch_open_meteo.py`.

## 2. Synthetic feeds (clearly labelled SYNTHETIC everywhere)

Generated by `sentinel/simulate.py`, seed 2026, one row per site cluster and day (12 × 1,049 = 12,588 rows).

| Feed | Expected daily count (before scenarios) |
|---|---|
| Heat-illness visits | per 10,000 on shift: 0.25 + 1.6 × exposure × susceptibility × acclimatisation × (exp(0.22·heat excess + 0.08·3-day heat excess) − 1) |
| Respiratory visits | per 10,000 on shift: 2.2 + 4.0 × (0.4 + outdoor share) × max(0, ln(PM10 lag-mean / 90)) |
| Other clinic visits | per 10,000 on shift: 14 |
| Incident reports | 0.18 × heat-illness rate + 0.15 per 10,000 |
| Sickness absence | binomial on workers on shift, rate 2.1% + heat, dust, holiday and scenario terms + noise |

- Heat excess = max(0, WBGT proxy − 30). Exposure = outdoor share × 0.7 during the UAE midday-break season (15 Jun – 15 Sep).
- Acclimatisation factor = 1 + 0.9 · exp(−hot days so far this year / 18), hot day = WBGT proxy ≥ 33.
- On-shift share: weekdays 100%, Friday 85%, Saturday 55%, Sunday 35%, Eid 25% (approximate Eid dates, ±1 day).
- Over-dispersion: gamma-Poisson with shape 8.

**Scenarios** (33, listed in `sentinel/config.py`): new unacclimatised crews (slow build), cooling-water or shelter failures (sudden, with complaints the day before), vulnerable crews in humid heat, dust surges (respiratory), and look-alikes (vaccination or screening days raising all-cause visits and absence only). Effects are additive in cases and scale with √(workforce / 4,000).

**Reporting mess** (`degrade_reporting`): clinic and incidents arrive 1 day late, absence 2 days; 2.5% of values missing at random; one 5-day clinic outage per site per year. True values are kept in `*_true` columns and used only for scoring.

## 3. Personal data

None. No individual records exist anywhere in the project. All health counts are synthetic and aggregated to site-cluster level.

## 4. Known gaps

- Site sizes and outdoor shares are illustrative.
- Effect sizes are assumptions in the published direction, not estimates.
- No archived operational forecasts; forecasts are reanalysis plus Gaussian error (WBGT 0.9 / 1.4; log PM10 0.35 / 0.50 for day 1 / day 2).
